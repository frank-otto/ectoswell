# Sounds – sources & licenses (Ectoswell)

All 130 sounds are real recordings released under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), reused from earlier games of this Halloween series (their source pages were checked for CC0 1.0 when they were first added). Nothing is synthesized – no oscillators, no beeps.

## Music – VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0 1.0)
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE – trimmed, faded and loudness-matched; the game re-pitches the nearest recorded note. Original music written for this game (a bouncy goo polka at 112 BPM and a menu nocturne), sequenced note by note.
